## Thee Oh Sees: Intercepted Message (2023)

Punky, synthy, and extremely seasoned take on weirdo nerd rock. Cools ideas, roided out double drummer drums. Thee Oh Sees hit a perfect area between obscurity and pop for me on this one.

<br/><br/><br/>

![Fuzz - Fuzz](/fuzz2.jpg)

## Fuzz: Fuzz (2013)

Guilty pleasure for cool kids, this alt supergroup sounds as dirty as they look.

<br/><br/><br/>




![Angine De Poitrine - Vol. 1](/Angine1.jpg)

## Angine De Poitrine: Vol. 1 (2024)

I sure hope Vol. 1 is a reference to Sabbath's Vol. 4, because it's a beautifully retarded homage to the path paved by the masters of reality. Creates an intriguing sensation that is as enjoyable as it is nerve-eating. Powering through half an hour of this very present duo isnt for every occassion or mood, it hits when it hits though.

<br/><br/><br/>

![Flux - Deca](/deca.jpg)

## Flux - Deca (2018)

Last and also least, still this album of hip hop beats is a refreshing take on an extremely oversaturated and dull elevator music scene.
